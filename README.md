# Qartello-E-Commerce-Business-Performance-Analytics-using-Power-BI


📌 **Project Overview**

Qartello is a fictional e-commerce business operating across multiple countries, product categories, brands, sellers, and sales channels. As the business grows, analyzing sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and operational efficiency becomes increasingly complex.

This project develops an interactive Power BI analytics solution that consolidates multiple datasets into a unified reporting environment, enabling management to monitor KPIs, identify performance trends, and make data-driven business decisions.

---

⚙️ **Problem Statement**

As Qartello expanded its e-commerce operations, fragmented business data limited visibility into sales, profitability, customer behavior, and operational performance. The organization needed a centralized analytics solution to track key metrics, identify improvement opportunities, and support strategic decision-making.

---

🎯 **Project Objectives**

- Integrate and transform data from multiple sources into a centralized Power BI reporting solution.

- Develop interactive dashboards to monitor key business KPIs and overall organizational performance.

- Analyze sales, profitability, customer behavior, products, sellers, and operational costs to identify trends and improvement opportunities.

- Improve data quality through cleaning, transformation, and validation.

- Enable data-driven decision-making through meaningful visualizations and actionable insights.

---

🛠️ **Tools & Technologies**

- Power BI Desktop – Interactive dashboards and reports

- Power Query – Data cleaning, transformation, and ETL

- DAX – KPI calculations, financial measures, and time-based analysis

- Data Modeling – Relationships, dimension tables, and fact tables

---

📊 **Dashboard Modules**

**1. Executive Dashboard**

Provides a consolidated overview of organizational performance through key financial, customer, and operational KPIs.

**Focus Areas:**

- Sales growth and net profit growth

- Net profit margin

- Repeat customer rate

- On-time delivery rate

- Revenue contribution by customer segment

- Sales and order trends by channel and region
  
---

**2. Sales Performance Dashboard**

Evaluates revenue trends and sales performance across brands, product categories, markets, and acquisition channels.

**Focus Areas:**

- Net sales and gross margin

- Year-over-year sales growth

- Monthly sales trends

- Brand and category performance

- Acquisition and order channel contribution

- Regional sales performance

---

**3. Operations Dashboard**

Monitors order fulfillment and delivery performance to identify operational inefficiencies.

**Focus Areas:**

- Total and delivered orders

- Cancellation and return rates

- Average delivery time

- On-time delivery rate

- Order channel performance

- Country-wise operational performance

---

**4. Product & Seller Dashboard**

Analyzes product profitability, brand contribution, and seller performance to support assortment and seller management decisions.

**Focus Areas:**

- Products and units sold

- Average selling price

- Top brands by net sales

- Seller performance and ratings

- Sales by seller type

- Category-level gross profit and margin

---

**5. Finance Dashboard**

Evaluates financial performance through a year-wise Profit & Loss statement and profitability analysis.

**Focus Areas:**

- Gross sales, discounts, and shipping revenue

- Net sales and cost of goods sold

- Gross profit and net profit

- Operating costs

- Gross and net profit margins

- Category and regional profitability

---

📈 **Key Business Insights**

- Strong Business Growth: Net sales reached $26.89M, with year-over-year sales growth of 47.34%.

- Improving Profitability: Net profit reached $11.92M, while the reported net profit growth was 51.04%.

- Leading Sales Channels: Website and Mobile App channels contributed the largest shares of order volume and net sales.

- Brand Concentration: Zenbyte and Nexa emerged as the leading brands by net sales.

- Category Profitability: Electronics generated the highest net sales, while several smaller categories recorded stronger margins.

- Operational Improvement Opportunity: The on-time delivery rate of 46.64% indicates a need to investigate delivery delays and improve fulfillment reliability.

- Customer Engagement: The reported repeat customer rate of 97.84% highlights the importance of maintaining customer satisfaction and retention.
